### How to interpret the shortlist

1. **4k–6k default comparison:** Maye+Doubs+Wilson+McBride has the highest mean in this *restricted* screen, but combines several highly popular players. The Hollins absence supports Doubs' role; his 7.5 targets are a subjective assumption.
2. **Alternative-quarterback emphasis:** Purdy+Kittle and Love+Watson are real pivot families. Purdy faces a formidable defense; Love with Swift and Odunze depends on a favorable pass-heavy CHI–GB game script, which is not guaranteed alongside increased Swift rushing.
3. **Gibbs isn't an automatic lock:** His workload and touchdown ceiling are attractive, but salary forces value WRs and Allen/other role-sensitive players. If those salary-release plays underperform, high-priced Gibbs can still leave the lineup behind. Keep the `maye_gibbs_mcbride` contender.
4. **Joint game failure sensitivity:** The Sunday runner discounts both Detroit and Arizona QB/pass-catcher/RB volume and touchdown components by 20% as one deterministic stress. It is *not* a forecast probability or the same as a correlated game simulation. Each candidate additionally gets a generic RB -20% workload/TD/bonus and selected-QB passing -20% diagnostic.

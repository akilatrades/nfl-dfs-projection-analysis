### B — Goff maximum game-stack bet

QB Jared Goff $6,600 · RB Braelon Allen $5,400 · RB D'Andre Swift $6,200 · WR Amon-Ra St. Brown $7,900 · WR Isaac TeSlaa $3,400 · WR Rome Odunze $4,700 · TE Trey McBride $6,700 · FLEX Michael Wilson $6,200 · DST Bears $2,900. **Total $50,000.**

Five DET–ARI players, including two Lions WR and two Arizona bring-backs. Intentionally omit Gibbs. TeSlaa at $3,400 is a volatility punt and not guaranteed to retain his recent targets. **He was not in the previously modeled 48-player Sunday pool; do not attach a fabricated combined projection to this lineup.** The published DraftKings Sunday value preview cites eight Week 4 targets, 95 receiving yards, a 64.4% Lions 11-personnel usage rate, but another modeling site estimated only ~4.4 DFS points for TeSlaa before the slate. The case is role and upper tail, not reliable median. To avoid the Browns DST versus NYJ RB Allen clash, the roster uses **Bears DST**, and roster choices don't include a Green Bay offensive player.

This is the **highest correlated DET–ARI game bet**, conditional on multiple offensive participants hitting together. If the game disappoints in efficiency or Detroit runs everything through Gibbs, it is particularly vulnerable.

## Ownership / scoring logic and evidence

- **Gibbs** ($9,800) is the expensive popular RB the market is clustering around. [Footballguys Oct 10 GPP breakdown](https://www.footballguys.com/article/2026-draftkings-gpp-domination-week-05) expects very heavy ownership but estimates differ across sites and this contest's actual ownership won't be known until lock.
- **DET–ARI** has the highest total of the main slate (sources vary ~53.5–54.5 as of Oct 10). Similar to last week's CIN–JAX miss, high total does **not** make a 50+ point outcome certain. A detached game-downside sensitivity remains in [Sunday Week 5 master research](README.md).
- **Romeo Doubs** may benefit from Mack Hollins [being placed on IR](https://www.reuters.com/sports/patriots-place-wr-mack-hollins-calf-injured-reserve--flm-2026-10-10/), but Chism and Douglas can also absorb targets. A prior manual Sunday assumption increased Doubs to 7.5 targets.
- **Braelon Allen** gets opportunity with [Hall confirmed out](https://www.reuters.com/sports/jets-rule-out-rb-breece-hall-quad-wr-adonai-mitchell-finger--flm-2026-10-09/); rainy Browns–Jets weather and a struggling Jets offense introduce real downside. Retain Allen as a conditional value option, not a must-play.
- **Isaac TeSlaa** is an add-on not yet calibrated by this project's model. [DraftKings Network Oct 11](https://dknetwork.draftkings.com/2026/10/11/nfl-dfs-value-picks-for-week-5-on-draftkings/) documents his $3,400 salary and recent opportunity. A boom-bust WR helps B's distinctive ownership profile but is not guaranteed a useful target share.

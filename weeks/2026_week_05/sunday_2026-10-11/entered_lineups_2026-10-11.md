## Why these are separate entries

Only Chase Brown and Chargers DST appear in both lineups. That's intentional: the $5 entry leans on Love–Watson and Arizona receiving volume; the $3 entry relies on Detroit passing production through Goff, St. Brown and LaPorta. Low player overlap doesn't mean the outcomes are independent, particularly because both builds have exposure to Detroit–Arizona.

## Why review it?

The earlier selected lineup had Jordan Love + Christian Watson and both Chicago RB D'Andre Swift and Chicago WR Rome Odunze. This is a **four-offensive-player concentration in the CHI–GB game**, total around 45.5 and Bears favored by about 1.5 on Sunday morning.

This is not inherently an invalid DFS tactic. But Swift’s rushing TD path and Odunze’s passing-volume/TD path can compete in lower-scoring scenarios. The strong case for each individually should not automatically mean **both** belong in a four-person secondary game stack.

- **Swift:** Monangai OUT; increased backfield share is reasonable, though exactly 18.5 modeled carries is subjective. Swift had limited injury practice earlier in week but returned fully by Friday.
- **Odunze:** Week 4 seven targets, 23% share and 89% snap participation, up from three targets in Week 1. That's credible role evidence, so removing him is *not* an assertion that he lacks ceiling.
- **Alternative:** Malik Washington costs $4,600, has 28 targets through four weeks, and creates independent exposure to MIA–CIN; Miami offense and quarterback Malik Willis are major negatives. The trade may reduce modeled mean, but reduces CHI–GB dependence.

# Prior-slate lessons applied to Week 10

This is a decision checklist with evidence, not a claim that the forecasting problem is solved.

| Prior evidence | Week 10 action | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Week 3: cheap Shough build lacked a compelling joint scoring story | Start with game/team environment, then compare QB families. Shough is paired with Olave and a credible Carolina opponent; Dak is paired with established Dallas targets. | Market totals do not guarantee passing touchdowns or their recipients. |
| Weeks 3–4: Coker/Parker opportunity assumptions exceeded evidence | Save roles and salary alternatives. Compare a Treadwell punt against builds without a thin WR. Final recommendations contain no WR below the screen's 9.5-point threshold. | That cutoff is a discretionary screen, not a validated predictor. |
| Week 4: ownership adjustment surrendered points; 8/11 players lacked inputs | Save every candidate's inputs, source labels, complete roster and metrics. Use ownership for field sensitivity, not a blanket projection haircut. | Some players have only one source; ownership for most is an analyst assumption. |
| Weeks 4–5: good game thesis, poor player/TD allocation | Allocate team passing/rushing TDs once among named players plus an unrostered residual. QB receives the same passing TD count as his team. | Non-TD scoring is an approximate score distribution, not target-by-target play simulation. |
| Week 5: Gibbs/Maye ceilings missed; unsupported cheap WR assumptions | Explicit Bijan and CMC forced-in comparisons; all 20 main-slate starting QBs are represented. Do not eliminate a QB because one cheap stack partner looks weak. | The 102-player pool is curated and is not the complete official DK pool. |
| Week 5: weak forced run-back and copied assumptions | Compare optional versus required run-backs. Do not require a San Francisco player in the Dallas construction. | No observed field proves a zero-run-back lineup is optimal. |
| Week 6: four Rams and weak secondary pieces | Maximum three offensive players from one team and four per game. Compare one and two TEs and single and double stacks. | These are sensible guardrails, not fitted optimal concentration limits. |
| Week 7: recommendation preceded promised reproducible work | Execute and save the candidate search, scoring simulations, sensitivities, field audits and exact recommendations before main-slate lock. | The recommendations are not evidence that the user entered those rosters. |
| Week 7: expensive Coker weak link; Titans DST arithmetic error | Audit usage and salary for all selected players; preserve DK scoring conventions and label the score model's approximation. | This model includes bonuses in forecast means rather than simulating exact yardage thresholds. |
| Week 8: Lamar/Flowers/Andrews joint collapse omitted from model | Explicit shared QB-failure stress: QB mean x .15, same-team WR/TE x .65, same-team RB x .90. Show this separately from normal scoring. No probability is assigned to that event. | Historical exit frequencies and backup-specific effects remain unfitted. This is a stress test, not an unconditional injury forecast. |
| Week 8: weak Andrews reintroduced to fit salary | No thin WR or cheap speculative second TE in the selected pair. Two-TE entry uses Warren/Kelce; other entry uses one TE/four WRs. | Warren's post-trade role is still uncertain and is stressed downward. |
| Week 8: late eligibility assumptions ignored returns | Verify official inactives; exclude Addison, Walker and other ruled-out players. Recognize Deebo/Allen competition, Cousins/McCord/Beck/Mendoza QB changes, and Downs' move to KC. | Afternoon inactives post after early lock. Friday clearance is not Sunday activation. |
| Week 8: too much weight on simulation rank; no contest validation | Compare full-capacity 5,882- and 12,254-entry hypothetical fields under multiple assumptions. Audit marginal ownership and opponent projection quality. Do not report synthetic first-place shares as real win odds. | No payout ladder, complete official salary export, real opponent field or multi-slate calibration. |
| Weeks 4–8: correlated portfolio and shared weak spots | Different QB/game constructions; only Emmett Johnson and Tyler Warren overlap. No shared thin WR punt. | Both can lose if those two value roles disappoint; diversification is incomplete by design. |
